Lesson 3: The Place Where the Sky Begins

Mara arrived at the old station just as the last light slipped behind the hills. The platform was empty, except for a suitcase turned on its side and a swallow beneath the glass roof.

She had travelled all day of the map. In her pocket, the letter from her grandmother had softened at its folds. It contained no address, only a sentence written in familiar blue ink: Follow the river until it remembers your name.

Beyond the tracks, the town rested in a blue-grey hush. A narrow path led through tall grass toward the water, and for the first time in years, Mara felt she was exactly where she needed to be.
